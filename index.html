A steady presence

You don't have to navigate the end of life alone.

Death can bring uncertainty, difficult decisions, profound emotion, and moments of extraordinary meaning. An end-of-life doula offers a calm and knowledgeable presence through that experience.

Light at the Threshold provides individualized, non-medical support for people approaching the end of life and for the families and friends who love them. Care is shaped around your needs, values, traditions, and beliefs.

About

Care centered on the person, not a particular path.

Every person approaches death differently. There is no single right way to experience it.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an end-of-life doula?

An end-of-life doula provides non-medical emotional, practical, and informational support to a dying person and the people around them.

Is a doula the same as hospice?

No. Hospice provides medical and interdisciplinary end-of-life care. A doula has a separate, non-medical role and can complement the care provided by hospice and other professionals.

Do I need to have a particular religious belief?

No. Support is available to people of all faiths, spiritual traditions, philosophies, and to people who do not identify with any religion or spiritual tradition.

When should someone contact a doula?

You do not need to wait until the final days of life. Some people reach out when a serious or terminal diagnosis is received, while others seek support much closer to death.
